# Design Decisions (ADRs)

This document records Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) — important technical decisions and the reasoning behind them.

> **Note**: This document focuses on design and architecture-specific decisions tied to the codebase. For the complete project-wide decision log (including non-technical decisions), see the [Team Decision Log](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jmIgUHrY35JjczQ1IYDKi7lT8bQ4WfQyifVkmHrpuFs/edit?gid=247725736#gid=247725736).

## Format

Each decision includes:
- **Title**: Clear description of the decision
- **Context**: Why this decision was needed
- **Decision**: What was decided
- **Rationale**: Why this decision was made
- **Consequences**: Pros and cons of this decision
- **Date**: When the decision was made
- **Status**: Active, Superseded, Deprecated, or Inactive
