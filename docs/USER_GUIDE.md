## User Profile

### Updating Your Profile

1. Click on your profile icon (top right)
2. Select **"Profile Settings"**
3. Update desired information:
   - Display name
   - Avatar/Profile picture
   - Bio
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Privacy Settings

### Change Password

1. Go to **Settings** → **Security**
2. Click **"Change Password"**
3. Enter current password
4. Enter new password (twice to confirm)
5. Click **"Update Password"**

## Troubleshooting

### Can't log in

**Problem**: "Invalid email or password"
- **Solution**: 
  - Verify email is correct
  - Check password (case-sensitive)
  - Use "Forgot Password" if unsure

**Problem**: "Account not verified"
- **Solution**:
  - Check your email for verification link
  - Resend verification email

### Can't see my progress

- Refresh the page (F5 or Cmd+R)
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Log out and log back in

### Performance issues

- Check internet connection
- Close other tabs/applications
- Clear browser cache
- Update your browser

## Account Management

### Logging Out

1. Click your profile icon
2. Select **"Log Out"**
3. You'll be returned to the login page

### Deleting Your Account

1. Go to **Settings** → **Account**
2. Scroll to bottom
3. Click **"Delete Account"**
4. Confirm by entering your password
5. Your account and data will be permanently deleted

⚠️ **Warning**: This action cannot be undone!
